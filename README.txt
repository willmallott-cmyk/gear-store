Gear Store update
1. Upload these 3 files to the repo root, replacing the existing ones:
   index.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest
2. Leave your icon files alone. Optionally delete the duplicate icon-192.PNG (keep icon-192.png).
3. Commit. After Pages redeploys, open the app and refresh once (close and reopen the home-screen app) to pick up the new service worker.
Existing data is migrated automatically; the old gearstore.v1 data is left untouched.
